Course descriptions

Select the courses you need. Complete Level 1 first, then take Levels 2–4 in any order to suit your work.

Level 1: Tekla Structures basic course

Tekla Structures basic course

Modelling, drawings and component basics

Who it’s for

For complete beginners and for engineers who know some Tekla tools but have not yet delivered a real project. Build a clear understanding of the workflow, including both modelling and drawing basics.

What you’ll learn and course outcomes

What you’ll learn

  • Navigate the interface, set up a model and organise your work.
  • Create and modify model objects using sound modelling practices.
  • Get an overview of components and how they support modelling.
  • Create basic drawings, add dimensions and prepare initial documentation.

After the course

Build a model and produce basic drawings using a consistent workflow, with a stronger foundation for your first real project.

Advanced

Level 2: Custom components in Tekla Structures

Custom components in Tekla Structures

Parametric custom components

Who it’s for

For engineers who have completed the basic course and want to automate repetitive modelling. Custom components are reusable building blocks with parameters—similar in principle to dynamic blocks in AutoCAD.

What you’ll learn and course outcomes

What you’ll learn

  • Create reusable custom components for connections and details.
  • Define dimensions, variables and rules to control component behaviour.
  • Make components adapt when model geometry or parameters change.
  • Apply and reuse your components across the structural model.

After the course

Model recurring connections more efficiently and reduce repetitive manual work when developing a complete 3D model.

Advanced

Level 3: Tekla Structures Advanced Training

Tekla Structures Advanced Training

Advanced modelling and drawing workflows

Who it’s for

For engineers who already work in Tekla Structures, often with 1–3 years of experience, but have not yet explored parametric modelling or the full range of drawing tools.

What you’ll learn and course outcomes

What you’ll learn

  • Use parametric tools and reusable solutions in everyday modelling.
  • Explore modelling features that improve speed and consistency.
  • Get more from drawing creation, editing and coordination with the model.
  • Refine your workflow using practical methods from real projects.

After the course

Benefit from our 16 years of practical Tekla experience and work more efficiently with both models and drawings.

Advanced

Level 4: System customisation and template creation

System customisation and template creation

Template Editor: reports and drawing tables

Who it’s for

For engineers who need reports and drawing tables that meet different clients’ documentation requirements. Focus on Tekla Template Editor, the tool for creating and adapting these templates.

What you’ll learn and course outcomes

What you’ll learn

  • Create and edit graphical templates for tables on drawings.
  • Build text templates for reports and material lists.
  • Work with template fields, layouts and formatting.
  • Adapt and check template output against client requirements.

After the course

Create and customise report templates and drawing tables supported by Tekla Structures, so you can meet each client’s documentation standards.

How the training works

Live demonstrations, guided exercises and time to practise.

  1. 1. Agree how to connect

    Before the course, your instructor contacts you using your preferred method. Together, you agree on a video-call service such as Zoom, Google Meet or another suitable platform.

  2. 2. Get started in Tekla Structures

    The course begins with an introduction. Your instructor explains how to launch Tekla Structures and helps you get ready to follow the lesson.

  3. 3. Watch, then follow along

    For each topic, your instructor demonstrates the tools and workflow. We normally use two monitors: one to watch the demonstration and the other to repeat the steps in Tekla Structures.

  4. 4. Put each topic into practice

    After each topic, you complete a short practical exercise to apply what you have learned.

Practice time depends on your course

Some topics need more hands-on work than others. Your instructor plans the time for exercises around the topics covered and your chosen course duration.

Before your training starts

Prepare your computer and connection before the first lesson.

Your instructor will contact you in advance using your preferred method to help you complete the setup. This preparation takes place before the course so that paid training time can be used for learning and practice.

  1. 1. Software and licences

    Your instructor helps you install Tekla Structures and activate the required licences. The software must be installed and ready to use on each participant’s own computer before the first lesson.

    We also agree in advance which plug-ins and additional software are needed for your company’s workflows.

  2. 2. Shared folders for groups

    If more than one participant is attending, your instructor helps set up shared folders so that the group can access the files needed for training.

  3. 3. Audio and video checks

    We check headphones and microphones, and cameras where needed. We also check that the agreed video-call service is working, to help avoid technical interruptions during the course.

Allow time for internal IT approvals

In larger organisations, approvals for software installation, shared-folder access and other IT permissions can take unexpectedly long because requests may need to pass through several departments. Please involve your IT team early and allow enough time to complete these steps before training starts.
